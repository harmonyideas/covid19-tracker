      About these numbers

      Cumulative confirmed cases and deaths from Johns Hopkins University's COVID-19 data, as of the last daily report in March 2023. Totals reflect testing and reporting at the time and do not show current spread.

      Counties with no count

      Some places, such as several Utah counties and Alaska boroughs, reported under a larger health district, so their own count shows as not reported.
