      Where the levels come from

      CDC's National Wastewater Surveillance System rates each treatment plant's COVID-19 viral activity every week. A county's level combines the plants that serve it, weighted by the number of people each plant serves. Counties with no reporting plant show no data.

      Case and death totals

      Counts stopped when national case reporting ended in March 2023. They are the final totals from The New York Times and do not reflect current spread.
